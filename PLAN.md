# Redesign plan

The site is where people Alejo wants to work with meet him. Every word on the key
pages and titles is chosen on purpose. The site stays minimal, with room for
small, cute details.

Words come before visuals, and each step is checked against the brief.

1. **Brief.** Who visits, what each visitor should do next, and what the site says
   in one sentence. Written in [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).
2. **Inventory.** Everything Alejo has made that others could use (templates,
   repos, prompts, posts), and the few that earn a pointer on the site.
3. **Words.** Several drafts of each key line (name line, section titles, pointer
   text); Alejo picks one of each. A short tone-of-voice note.
4. **References.** 15–30 sites, type samples and objects Alejo likes, each with a
   note on what Alejo likes about it.
5. **Visual directions.** Three clearly different directions, each with a type
   pairing, palette, one recurring graphic idea and a motion style, shown as the
   real homepage with the real words. Pick one, or combine two.
6. **System.** Short written rules for the chosen direction: type sizes, spacing,
   colours, and how links and pointers look.
7. **Structure and build.** The fewest pages that do the job, built in this repo.
8. **Polish.** One pass per dimension: typography, spacing, hover and click
   states, motion, mobile, then favicon, link preview image, 404 page and small
   hidden jokes.
9. **Launch.** Merge into `main`, deploy, and reread the site after a week.

[CURRENT-CONTENT.md](CURRENT-CONTENT.md) holds the previous site's text as raw
material.
