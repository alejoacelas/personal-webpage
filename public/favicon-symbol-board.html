Favicon symbol board

Twelve compact marks for alejoacelas.com, shown at real favicon scales and in a browser tab context. The priority is recognizability at 16px, then fit with the site's organic notes interface.

deep blue / identity anchor
sage / organic signal
brick / human warmth
