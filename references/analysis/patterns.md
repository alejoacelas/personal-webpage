# Patterns in the favourites

First pass, drawn from the draft notes, the measured type and colour
([tokens.md](tokens.md)) and the details catalogue ([details.md](details.md)),
before Alejo's own notes. Redo after he annotates the favourites.

## What most favourites share

- **Reading size and measure.** Body text is 16–20 px (median 16), line height
  about 1.5 times the size, and about 80 characters per line (most between 55
  and 105).
- **A light page with one accent.** 36 of 46 sites are light: 20 on plain
  white, the rest mostly warm off-white (#faf9f5, #fffcf2, #fdf6f3). The accent
  is usually a red or orange: Roots of Progress #b92d24, Forethought #ff6f43,
  Goodfire #c8622e, Vesuvius #f2542d. About eight are dark, among them Bret
  Victor, Frank Chimero, Gordon Brander, Stripe Press and Linear Method.
- **Serif and sans in equal measure.** 22 of 46 use a serif somewhere and 15 set
  body text in one. The rest are sans throughout. Where both appear, the serif
  carries reading or titles and a sans or monospace carries labels, dates and
  navigation.
- **Lists of work that carry their own context.** The commonest structure is an
  index: dated items, a one-line gloss each, and the important ones made bigger.
- **Small uppercase or monospace metadata.** Kickers, dates, counts and section
  numbers are set small and apart from the reading text.
- **A hand-made mark.** A drawn portrait, a line illustration, handwriting or a
  paper texture appears on about a third of the favourites, usually once per page.
- **Candour in the words.** Many state plainly what something is or isn't:
  METR's "rough/unpolished", Arcadia's shelved projects, Are.na's "no ads", Bret
  Victor's "goes pretty deep".

## Where the favourites split

1. **Quiet page or magazine.** One narrow column of small type and no images
   (Paco Coursey, Emil Kowalski, Thinking Machines, Tufte CSS, Gordon Brander), or
   an editorial layout with illustration, colour blocks and mixed type (Works in
   Progress, Asterisk, Orion, Roots of Progress, Public Domain Review).
2. **Research lab or handmade.** Graph paper, figures, monospace metadata and a
   dated index (Forethought, Goodfire, Transformer Circuits, Physical
   Intelligence, METR), or playful and personal: doodles, handwriting, jokes
   (Nicky Case, Jacky Zhao, Lynn Fisher, Henry Desroches, Brad Woods, Kinopio).
3. **Light or dark.** Most favourites are light; a smaller group is dark and
   austere (Bret Victor, Frank Chimero, Gordon Brander, Linear Method).

## Three directions these suggest

Each is mocked up as a real homepage in [../directions/](../directions/).

- **A. Lab notebook.** A light serif for headlines and monospace for everything
  else, on warm off-white with a faint grid. One rust accent. The work is a dated
  log, each entry with a one-line gloss, and a stamped "in progress" label on
  anything unfinished. From Forethought, Goodfire, Physical Intelligence,
  Transformer Circuits and METR Notes.
- **B. Quiet page.** One narrow column of small sans on white. A "Now" line,
  titles with a grey one-line description, a year stamp after each item, and
  italic serif only for a few emphasised words. Nothing else. From Paco Coursey,
  Emil Kowalski, Celine Nguyen, Bret Victor and Jenny Odell.
- **C. Small magazine.** High-contrast serif titles, monospace bylines and
  labels, a pen-drawn portrait beside the name, and the work laid out as a table
  of contents with kickers. One colour block for the call to email. From Works
  in Progress, Asterisk, Roots of Progress, Orion and Dense Discovery.
