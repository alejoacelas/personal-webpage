# Small details in the favourites

Touches that recur across the 46 favourites, grouped by what they do. Each lists
the sites that use it. Drafted from screenshots before Alejo's notes; redo after.

## Saying how finished something is

- **A label that says the work is rough.** METR's rubber-stamp "NOTE: unpolished,
  less reviewed"; Brad Woods' seed / sprout / mature / decay status per note;
  Arcadia's public "Icebox" of shelved projects, each tagged with why it stopped.
- **Planted and tended dates.** Brad Woods shows when a note was started and last
  revised; Paco, Emil Kowalski and Celine Nguyen keep a "Now", "Today" or
  "This year" line.
- **A version number for the site itself.** Lynn Fisher ("v. XIX"), Henry
  Desroches ("VER. SS25").

## Lists of work that carry their own context

- **A dated index as the main structure.** Bret Victor (every project since 1999
  in a year column), Tom MacWright (ISO dates right-aligned), Gordon Brander
  (YYYY.MM.DD), Transformer Circuits (month stamp in the gutter), Physical
  Intelligence (dotted vertical timeline), Melting Asphalt (MM/DD archive).
- **A one-line gloss after each title.** Bret Victor ("first light", "goes pretty
  deep"), Emil Kowalski and Paco (grey description under each item), Jenny Odell
  (italic line under each piece), Frank Chimero (type and years under each
  section).
- **Big entries and small entries.** Transformer Circuits gives major papers a
  headline and figure and minor updates one line; Physical Intelligence boxes
  major releases with a hard black shadow; Bret Victor highlights his best work
  in yellow.
- **Client or venue credit on each item.** Shirley Wu ("→ for The Guardian"),
  Visual Cinnamon, Jenny Odell ("(2025, audio + transcript)").

## Labels and metadata

- **Monospace for metadata, serif for reading.** Works in Progress, Physical
  Intelligence, Goodfire, Anthropic, Linear Method, Vesuvius Challenge.
- **Small uppercase kicker above a title.** Asterisk ("INTERVIEW"), Orion
  ("ANIMALIA"), Long Now, Convergent Research, Linear Method.
- **Numbered sections and notes.** Linear Method (2.1, 2.2), Alexander Obenauer
  ("LN 037"), julian.digital ("01 INTRO"), Dense Discovery (issue numbers).
- **A mark on links that leave the site.** "↗" on Paco and Frank Chimero; a chart
  icon on OWID links that open a chart.

## Hand-made marks

- **A drawn portrait or logo.** Works in Progress (pen portrait beside the
  wordmark), Nicky Case (cartoon peeking over the page), METR (pencil-sketch
  author portraits), Alexander Obenauer (flask).
- **Line drawings instead of photos.** Forethought's orange line fans, Ink &
  Switch's line-art letterforms, Long Now's curly-brace timeline, Linear's dashed
  circles, Convergent's orbital lines.
- **Handwriting in a typed page.** Jacky Zhao's handwritten dates and sign-off,
  julian.digital's "Bottleneck" note with an arrow, Giorgia Lupi's red
  corrections.
- **Paper surfaces.** Graph-paper grid (Forethought, Goodfire), ruled notebook
  with a red margin (METR), paper grain (Lynn Fisher), kraft paper (Brad Woods).

## Something live

- **A countdown or counter.** Dense Discovery ("next issue in 5 days"), Vesuvius
  ("234 days left"), Are.na ("15 years and 87 days").
- **A ticker.** Henry Desroches (local time, weather, now playing), Giorgia Lupi,
  Brad Woods, Kinopio.
- **Who else is here.** Jacky Zhao's arrivals board of live visitors.

## Asides and jokes

- **Wry names for ordinary things.** Bret Victor ("Bits & blurts."), Nicky Case
  ("toss monies at me"), Jacky Zhao ("The oak letter desk"), Melting Asphalt
  ("Needs some love").
- **Small honest promises.** Nicky Case ("max 1 update per month"), Kinopio ("no
  ads, tracking, or AI bloat"), Are.na ("No ads, no personalized
  recommendations").
- **Secrets.** Kinopio ("Other secret features may be available… (don't tell
  anyone)"), Stripe Press's lone "?" in the margin.

## Reading aids

- **Sticky table of contents with the current section marked.** Thinking
  Machines, Brad Woods (with a progress bar).
- **Copy link and Cite buttons.** METR, OWID, Forethought.
